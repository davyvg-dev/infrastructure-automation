# Logo designer brief — Klantkraan

> One-page brief. Paste this into a Fiverr Pro / 99designs / Upwork project as-is. Source-of-truth for everything below is `09-brand/visual-identity-brief.md`. **Read it before deviating.**

## Project at a glance

- **Brand**: Klantkraan — productized AI-receptionist + missed-call automation for Dutch trades (plumbers, roofers, electricians, heating installers).
- **Target audience for the logo**: Dutch tradespeople (loodgieters, dakdekkers, elektriciens, installateurs), 1–5 staff, mid-30s to mid-50s. They distrust slick / tech-bro aesthetics. They trust plain, honest, well-engineered things.
- **Use cases**: marketing website header (4:1), favicon (16×16, 32×32), app icon (1024×1024), social avatar (1:1), email signature (6:1), van sticker (long-form).
- **Budget**: €100–300 fixed-price. €1,500+ engagement is deferred to month 9.
- **Timeline**: 5 working days from brief acceptance.
- **License**: Full copyright transfer to T4 Software Consulting BV. No "designed by" credit required.

## Deliverables

1. **Wordmark** (primary mark) in 3 colour variants:
   - Primary: `#0F4C81` (kraan-blue) on `#FAF6EE` (cream) — see § 3 of `visual-identity-brief.md`
   - Mono-black on white
   - Mono-white on `#0F4C81`
2. **Optional pictogram** (favicon-friendly mark) — 2–3 explorations. Pick **one** direction from § Direction below.
3. **Lockup**: wordmark + pictogram side-by-side (4:1 ratio).
4. **Files**:
   - SVG masters (wordmark, pictogram, lockup × 3 variants each)
   - PNG @ 1024×1024 (transparent + cream bg versions)
   - ICO favicon (16, 32, 48 multi-resolution)
   - WOFF2 of any custom-spaced font version (if you ship a customised glyph)
5. **One-page PDF style guide**: spacing rules, minimum sizes, what NOT to do.

## Wordmark constraints (non-negotiable)

- **Set in `Inter Tight` Semibold (600)**, all lowercase. Custom letter-spacing `-0.02em`. Source: https://rsms.me/inter/ (SIL OFL).
- No serif, no script, no italic.
- No tagline lock-up — taglines change too often (see `01-strategy/positioning.md` H2 variations).
- Must read clearly at **16 px** (favicon proximity).
- Pure black + pure white variants must work too. No gradients ever.
- No drop shadow. No outline. No stroke.

## Pictogram direction — pick ONE

| Direction | Description | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The valve** | A circular tap-valve / stopcock simplified to 4–6 lines. Reads as a tap or a thermostatic valve. | Can read as a Wi-Fi icon if oversimplified. |
| **B. The K-tap** | A lowercase `k` whose lower diagonal turns into a tap spout. Type-glyph hybrid. | Hardest to execute well. Only pick this if your portfolio shows type-glyph skill. |
| **C. The flow** | A short curved line emerging from a square base, drop at the end. | Most generic — easy escape if A and B fail. |

**Recommended attempt**: A and C, two versions of each. Skip B unless you have type-glyph hybrid samples in your portfolio.

## Forbidden — automatic reject

- ❌ A literal photo-realistic kraan (faucet)
- ❌ A water drop with a face / mascot
- ❌ A circuit-board pattern (the "tech-ness" of the product is invisible — don't surface it)
- ❌ A speech bubble (this is not a chat app)
- ❌ A megaphone (this is not a marketing agency)
- ❌ Sparkles, stars, AI-sparkle iconography
- ❌ A gradient
- ❌ A drop shadow
- ❌ More than two colours
- ❌ Registered-trademark symbol — BOIP filing not registered yet (will be added later)
- ❌ Anything that screams "Silicon Valley pastel" or "Hormozi neon-orange-on-black"

## Tone reference (visual feel we're aiming for)

- **Wim Crouwel grid heritage** — Dutch-functional, rectilinear confidence.
- **Hansgrohe / Geberit catalogues** — utilitarian industrial design rendered cleanly.
- **Plain.com, Basecamp.com** — anti-trendy B2B restraint.
- **NOT** Linear (too SaaS-glossy), NOT Notion (too whimsical), NOT Stripe (too sophisticated for trades).

## Colour usage in the logo

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--kraan-blue` | `#0F4C81` | Primary brand colour. Wordmark and pictogram default. |
| `--kraan-cream` | `#FAF6EE` | Background. **NOT** to be used inside the mark — only behind it. |
| `--kraan-rust` | `#C75A2B` | **DO NOT** use in the logo. Reserved for accent moments in the marketing site only. |

Two-colour version: kraan-blue + a single shade of itself (e.g. `#0a3960` darker), nothing else.

## Acceptance criteria

Before payment, the deliverable must:

1. Render legibly at **16 px** on cream and on white (favicon test).
2. Survive being printed at 2 cm wide on a van sticker without breaking up.
3. Pass WCAG 4.5:1 contrast on cream and on white when shown in primary kraan-blue.
4. Include the SVG masters — not just PNG.
5. Include a 1-page PDF style guide (spacing, minimum size, what NOT to do).

Reject (politely) and request revision if any of the forbidden items show up, or if the mark can't survive single-colour rendering.

If you want to bridge the gap with a free placeholder before a paid designer turns this around, try these prompts in **v0.dev** or **Recraft**. Treat the output as throwaway — replace within month 3.

### v0.dev prompt (paste verbatim)

```
Design a minimalist wordmark logo for a Dutch B2B brand called "klantkraan".

CONSTRAINTS:
- Wordmark only, all lowercase, set in Inter Tight Semibold with -0.02em letter-spacing
- Single colour: deep blue #0F4C81 on a warm cream background #FAF6EE
- No gradient, no shadow, no outline, no decorative flourish
- Must read clearly at 16px (favicon proximity)
- Optional: a tiny pictogram to the left of the wordmark that reads as a tap-valve or stopcock — circular, 4-6 lines max, geometric, NOT a literal faucet, NOT a Wi-Fi shape
- Aesthetic reference: Wim Crouwel + Hansgrohe industrial design + plain.com restraint
- Anti-reference: do NOT make it look like a tech startup, do NOT add sparkles or AI iconography

Output 4 variations on a single canvas.
```

### Recraft prompt (paste verbatim)

```
A minimalist vector wordmark for a Dutch trade-services SaaS called "klantkraan", set in Inter Tight Semibold lowercase, kraan-blue #0F4C81 on a cream background #FAF6EE. Beside the wordmark, a small geometric pictogram that reads as a tap-valve or stopcock — circular, 4-6 clean lines, no literal faucet imagery, no Wi-Fi shape. Anti-trendy, Dutch-functional, Wim Crouwel grid heritage. No gradient, no shadow, no decorative flourish. Vector / SVG-ready.
```

### Quick acceptance test for AI output

1. Does it render at 16px without becoming illegible mush? (zoom out in the preview)
2. Is the pictogram a tap-valve or a Wi-Fi/circle/dot blob?
3. Does it have a gradient or shadow it shouldn't?
4. Could you put it on a Dutch tradesman's van sticker without him cringing?

If it passes all four, use as a placeholder. If not, iterate or skip straight to a Fiverr Pro brief using the body of this document.
