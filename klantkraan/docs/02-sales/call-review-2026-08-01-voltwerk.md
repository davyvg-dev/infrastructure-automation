# Call review — LinkedIn electrician prospect (voltwerk), 31 Jul evening

First recorded cold call (4:14, via colleague Israel). Felt bad from the inside; was
actually a decent call with one expensive improvisation and a soft close. Demo config
live at `demo.klantkraan.nl/?client=voltwerk` (placeholder brand).

## Verdict

Structure followed (opener → context → discovery → value → offer → next step), honest
throughout, ended with permission to send the demo. Three fixes for the next dial.

## 1. Improvised a new offer on the spot (2:03) — the expensive one

Said: **eerste maand gratis, tweede maand €150, daarna €300/mnd.** The decided offer is
€149 first month then €299 — no free month (free = "worth nothing" positioning, decided
against in competitor research). Also inverted the guarantee ("geld terug als u tevreden
bent").

**Resolution: honor the promised terms for THIS prospect** (word > €150), put them in
writing in the follow-up so there is no drift, and never improvise pricing again. The
belsheet says _niets anders aanbieden_ — that line exists for exactly this moment.

## 2. Argued with the discovery answer instead of digging (0:55)

Prospect: "die worden onmiddellijk beantwoord... ik ben bezig, maar ik heb het ook
opgenomen" — claims no pain, answers everything mid-job. Response told him he does have
pain ("dit hoor ik vaker..."). Better: one more question so he finds it himself:

- "Wat kost dat u — de klus stilleggen voor elk telefoontje?"
- "En als u op vakantie bent, of 's nachts?"
- "Hoeveel belletjes zijn kleine vragen die uw tijd niet waard zijn?"

If genuinely no pain: qualify out politely.

## 3. Soft close (3:26)

Prospect: wants to "overleggen" + look "op rustige momenten" (classic brush-off). Call
ended "ik stuur de link en we houden contact" — no date, no time. Fix costs one sentence:
"Ik app 'm nu meteen — mag ik u donderdag even terugbellen, eind van de middag of begin
van de avond?"

## What worked (keep)

- Discovery question asked before pitching (0:45).
- Honest that voice doesn't exist yet (3:07), pivoted to missed-call → WhatsApp catcher.
- Friendly, unhurried; mirrored the prospect's switch to "je"; warm ending.
- "Elke lead komt bij u op de telefoon binnen, u mist er geen één meer" landed clean.

## Never used (free ammo)

- AI Act hook: verplicht vanaf 2 aug — "die van ons doet het al netjes."
- The cap: "tien oprichtersklanten, tot 30 september" (came out garbled).

## Next action

Send the voltwerk demo link with the promised terms in writing + the Thursday question in
the same message. Log outcome in pipeline.
