## Automation decision (after pilot 1, revisit after pilot 3)

Automate a stage only when the log shows it is (a) founder-time heavy AND (b) mechanical.
The research predicts stage 2 is the automation win and stages 1/4/5 stay human. Confirm or
correct with the numbers above before building any generator.
