# Positioning

## One-line positioning

> Klantkraan is the Dutch-language, EU-hosted "klantenmotor" for installateurs en loodgieters — one fixed-price stack that turns every missed call, finished job and Google review into a booked afspraak. Geen setup, maandelijks opzegbaar.

## Category we play in

Category creation: **"klantenmotor"** (customer engine). Not "AI receptionist" (commoditised), not "marketing automation" (vague), not "leadgeneratie" (associated with pay-per-lead marketplaces tradesmen distrust). Klantenmotor frames the product as the engine that turns existing demand into bookings — which is exactly what Dutch tradesmen need (they have demand, they leak it).

## White space we own

From the competitor research, the gap is real:

| Competitor type | What they sell | What they miss |
|---|---|---|
| NL voice-only (Cowcierge €150, Voicelabs €149, Flireo €0.07/min) | One AI module | No bundle, no vertical scripts, no review engine |
| NL bundled (Gold Lemon €199+€899, MyAutoPilot €350+€1,350) | Multi-module SMB stack | Horizontal, not trade-vertical, setup fees |
| NL vertical (InstallatieTelefoniste) | Trade-aware AI phone | Single module (voice only) |
| Lead marketplaces (Werkspot, Gigaleads) | Pay-per-lead | Tradesmen resent these; not the customer's own pipeline |
| UK Invox (£99) | Vertical voice + reviews | UK only, not Dutch-native, no EU hosting story |
| US Podium ($399) | Full bundle | 12-mo lock-in, not Dutch, not GDPR-native |

**Our wedge:** the only Dutch player with **all five** of:
1. Trade-vertical (loodgieters + dakdekkers)
2. Bundled (3 modules in one price)
3. EU-hosted, AVG-native, AI Act-compliant
4. No setup fee, monthly cancel
5. Tariff-aware Dutch scripts (voorrijkosten, spoedtoeslag, materiaalopslag)

## Three differentiation pillars

### 1. **Dutch-vakman-native**
The AI script knows what `voorrijkosten`, `spoedtoeslag`, `materiaalopslag` mean. It uses ABN Dutch with no Anglicismen. It addresses callers with `u`, switches to `je` when the caller does. It mentions "binnen 4 uur terugbellen" — not "we'll get back to you shortly." This is a moat because building it requires understanding the trade, not just translating an English prompt.

### 2. **EU AI Act + AVG out of the box**
By August 2026, Article 50 transparency obligations are in force across the EU. Every voice agent must disclose at call start. Klantkraan ships with the disclosure baked in, EU-only data residency (Hetzner Frankfurt + Neon EU + Cloudflare EU Workers), and a signed DPA + DPIA template the client can show to their own customers if challenged. Foreign tools won't have this for at least 6–12 months.

### 3. **Skin in the game**
- No setup fee
- Monthly cancel
- First month 50% off
- 30-day money-back guarantee
- Public demo number anyone can call right now

This destroys risk for the buyer. Competitors that lock in for 12 months can't follow without losing margin.

## What we're NOT (positioning don'ts)

- ❌ Not an "AI agency". Tradesmen distrust agencies.
- ❌ Not a "lead-gen service". Werkspot owns that smell.
- ❌ Not a "marketing platform". That implies dashboards they won't open.
- ❌ Not "Powered by GPT" or any model-name. Buyers don't care; the technology is invisible.
- ❌ Not "Save 80% on staff costs". Wrong frame — most tradesmen don't have a receptionist to replace.

## Frames we use

### Anti-frame (the problem)
- "U mist 28% van uw oproepen — dat zijn 2-3 klussen per week."
- "Uw concurrent neemt 24/7 op. U niet."
- "Eén misgelopen badkamer-renovatie is meer dan een jaar Klantkraan."

### Pro-frame (the solution)
- "De Klantenmotor voor loodgieters."
- "Elke gemiste oproep teruggebeld binnen 60 seconden."
- "Reviews, terugbel-afspraken, en een AI-receptionist die uw vak spreekt."

## Brand voice (full doc: `09-brand/voice-and-tone.md`)

- Direct, no "wij staan voor onze klanten centraal" fluff
- BTW-inclusive prijzen waar mogelijk
- Echte naam + KVK + 06-nummer altijd zichtbaar
- Doe-maar-gewoon (Hofstede masculinity 14): geen superlatieven, geen "revolutionair"
- Tradesmen are time-poor: every sentence delivers

## Geographic positioning

- **Year 1 — NL primary**: 90% of marketing in Dutch, 10% English on LinkedIn for build-in-public reach.
- **Year 1 H2 — UK pilot**: separate `klantkraan.co.uk` (or rebrand for UK if testing shows the name doesn't travel), English Synthflow voice, English Smartlead campaign. Same product, different config.
- **Year 2 — Spain**: deferred. Spanish-language voice quality + local proof + payment rails (SEPA + Bizum) all require dedicated investment.
