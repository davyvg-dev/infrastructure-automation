# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Primary persona: Dutch loodgieter, owner-operator

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Business type | BV (filter for outbound) or eenmanszaak (inbound + LinkedIn only) |
| Size | 2–8 staff including owner |
| SBI codes | 4322 (loodgieters, sanitair, gas, verwarming) |
| Geography | Randstad start (Zuid-Holland holds ~20% of all NL plumbers): Den Haag, Rotterdam, Utrecht, Amsterdam, Leiden, Delft, Gouda. Expand to Brabant + Gelderland month 3. |
| Average job value | €150–600 emergency, €1,200 avg billable job |
| Inbound call volume | 15–60 calls/week |
| Missed-call rate | 27–40% (industry data: Aira/Invoca/411 Locals) |
| After-hours share | ~68% of plumbing emergencies are after-hours |
| Tech stack today | Mostly manual: paper werkbon or basic tool (Snelstart, Skoon, Werkbon). Google Business Profile underused. |
| Pain ranked | (1) missed calls = lost jobs, (2) slow quote follow-up, (3) thin Google reviews, (4) being "the bottleneck" themselves |
| Buying psychology | Direct, skeptical of marketing, references > testimonials, monthly cancel mandatory, BTW-inclusive pricing required |
| Decision authority | Owner alone (Hofstede individualism 80) |
| Sales cycle | 7–21 days when price < €1k/mo |
| Willingness to pay | High — one recovered emergency call (€450–600) covers the monthly fee |

### Where they spend attention

- **WhatsApp** (primary), local Facebook groups (regional vakman/klusbedrijf groepen)
- LinkedIn personal: low frequency (1–2x/week) but rising for 35–55 owners
- Trade media: `installatie.nl`, `cobouw.nl`, `vakbladwarmtepompen.nl`
- Trade associations: **Techniek Nederland** (~30k members), **OnderhoudNL**, **Uneto-VNI** (merged into Techniek Nederland)

## Secondary persona: Dutch dakdekker, owner-operator

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 2–10 staff |
| SBI codes | 4391 (dakdekkers / dakbedekking) |
| TAM | ~4.7k–6.3k businesses (BoldData, Companydata) |
| Average job value | €4,500 repair → €30,000 full replacement |
| Sales cycle | Long, quote-heavy. Speed-to-quote is the lever. |
| Agency saturation | LOW — no Dutch specialist agency found in research |
| Pain ranked | (1) slow quote response loses deals, (2) no system for follow-up after site survey, (3) seasonal whiplash (autumn peak) |
| Willingness to pay | High — one recovered €15k replacement = 25× the monthly fee |

Dakdekkers tolerate a higher ticket and a longer onboarding because the asymmetry of one closed quote is so large. Same product, different system-prompt tariff fields, different LP messaging.

## Avoid (do not target in year 1)

| Niche | Why avoid |
|---|---|
| **HVAC / warmtepomp installateurs** | 12–16 week backlogs in 2026; they reject leads, not seek them. Most saturated agency segment (VPM, Cherry, Gigaleads, Brutael). |
| Schilders | Low job value (€1.5–5k), heavy referral, price-sensitive, low margin for €599/mo. |
| Schoonmaakbedrijven | Contract-based already; missed-call recovery doesn't move the needle. |
| Slotenmakers | TAM too small (a few hundred). Won't scale. |

## Lookalike + tertiary verticals (month 6+)

If loodgieter + dakdekker proven by month 6, expand to:

1. **Hoveniers** (~15k NL businesses, €5–15k jobs) — heavy seasonality risk; consider an annual-prepay-only tier.
2. **Elektriciens** (~15k) — mix of project + emergency; medium pain. Test after loodgieter playbook proven.
3. **UK plumbers** — same product, English Synthflow voice, separate Smartlead campaign. Invox is the incumbent benchmark.

## ICP-fit qualification questions (for sales)

1. Hoeveel inkomende calls per week, ruwe schatting?
2. Welk percentage pak je niet op — eerlijk?
3. Werk je alleen of met monteurs?
4. BV of eenmanszaak? *(determines outbound legality if cold-acquired)*
5. Wat is een gemiddelde klus aan omzet?
6. Postcodes waar je werkt?
7. Wie beslist over zo'n investering — jij alleen?

Disqualify if: ≤1 call/week, full-time employee (geen owner-operator), schimmel verleden (klacht-databases via Belastingdienst Vakvereniging), or budget < €200/mo absolute cap.
