# Content Channel Strategy

> Faceless brand, but founder posts under real name (research showed personal profiles get 2.75x impressions vs company pages — the trade-off of strict company-only is too costly). Dutch 90%, English 10%.

## Channel ROI ranking

| Rank | Channel                                                                       | Time-to-first-result               | Why                                                                            |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | **LinkedIn personal** (founder, no face)                                      | 4–8 weeks for first inbound DMs    | Algorithm rewards humans 2.75x over companies. Owner-operators read 1–2x/week. |
| 2    | **Local SEO / Google Business Profile**                                       | 4–10 weeks for map-pack visibility | Trades search "[dienst] [stad]" with high commercial intent. Cheap moat.       |
| 3    | **Blog / SEO (Dutch problem-keyword)**                                        | 3–6 months                         | Low-competition Dutch long-tail. Compounds.                                    |
| 4    | **Email newsletter**                                                          | 2–4 weeks once list >100           | Owned channel. Doubles as outbound warm-up.                                    |
| 5    | **YouTube long-form (screen-recordings)**                                     | 3–6 months                         | Ranks in Google. Lives as a sales asset.                                       |
| 6    | **LinkedIn company page**                                                     | n/a                                | Reach ~1.6% of followers. Keep alive for social proof only.                    |
| 7    | **YouTube Shorts**                                                            | 1–3 months                         | Free repurposing of long-form.                                                 |
| 8    | **Niche communities** (higherlevel.nl, Reddit r/thenetherlands, trade forums) | 2–6 weeks                          | High trust, manual scale.                                                      |
| 9    | **Podcast guesting**                                                          | 2–4 months                         | Be a guest on MKB/Bouwend Nederland podcasts. Don't host your own.             |
| 10   | Instagram                                                                     | Skip in Phase 1                    | Low B2B intent for trades                                                      |

## Faceless content patterns — what converts

### Use these

- **Screen-recordings of real automations** (Loom + captions) — "Hier draait een AI-receptionist voor een loodgieter in Utrecht — luister mee."
- **Before / after dashboards** — Figma/Excalidraw graphics. Cheap, persuasive.
- **Animated case studies** — Canva + CapCut + ElevenLabs Dutch voice-over.
- **LinkedIn carousels** — "Waarom installatiebedrijven 30% van offertes verliezen" — top organic format in NL B2B.

### Avoid these

- **HeyGen / Synthesia / Argil talking-head avatars in Dutch** — Dutch viewers detect the accent. Trust collapses.
- **Auto-generated stock B-roll Reels with TTS robot voice** — looks like spam.
- **"ChatGPT wrote this" carousels** — Dutch market explicitly rewards directness, no fluff.

## Dutch vs English split

**90% Dutch, 10% English.**

Why:

- Trade-business owners read Dutch. English signals "not for me."
- "AI voor installateurs", "CRM loodgieter", "offertesoftware monteur" — these long-tail keywords are uncrowded.
- UK expansion = rewrite ~20 cornerstone pieces, not a strategy pivot.

The 10% English: founder's LinkedIn "build-in-public" posts — these get reshared internationally and seed the UK audience for Phase 2.

## Founder posting policy (LinkedIn personal)

- **Identity**: real name, real headshot is NOT required (faceless brand — use a Klantkraan logo as avatar, or an abstract minimal avatar).
- **Headline**: "Bouw automatiseringen voor Nederlandse installateurs. Klantkraan." → states what + who + where.
- **About section**: 3 paragraphs. The problem (missed calls). The product (AI receptionist + reviews + SMS). The proof (case studies, even early ones).
- **Voice**: direct, no Anglicismen, "doe maar gewoon."

## Cadence (10–15h/week founder budget)

| Channel                                     | Frequency     | Time per item                                      |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| LinkedIn personal posts                     | 5/week        | 20 min each (mostly Claude-drafted, founder edits) |
| LinkedIn company reposts                    | 1/week        | 5 min                                              |
| YouTube long-form (screen-rec)              | 1 / 2 weeks   | 90 min including edit                              |
| YouTube Shorts                              | 3/week        | 15 min each (repurposed from long-form)            |
| Blog cornerstone (Dutch)                    | 1/week        | 90 min including SEO                               |
| Newsletter                                  | 1/week        | 30 min (repurposes top LinkedIn post)              |
| Google Business Profile posts               | 1/day weekday | 5 min each                                         |
| Community comments (higherlevel.nl, Reddit) | 5/week        | 10 min each                                        |

Total weekly: ~9 hours of content production. Within budget.

## What drives leads (vs. what drives reach)

| High reach, low lead        | High lead, lower reach                          |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Hot takes / "controversial" | Specific case studies with €-numbers            |
| Listicles                   | Tutorial screen-recordings                      |
| Personal stories            | "I built this for [client] — here's the result" |
| Industry commentary         | "Het misverstand over [X]" + how to fix         |

Plan for both: hot takes Mondays for reach, case studies Wednesdays for leads.
