A phone system with 350,000 business customers now bundles an AI receptionist for free.

That is the product I sell. And I think it is the best thing that could have happened to my company.

Here is why.

Answering the phone was never the hard part. For a Dutch roofer or plumber, the money leaks after hello: the quote that never gets chased, the no-show that wastes a crew's morning, the invoice that sits unpaid for six weeks, the happy customer nobody asks for a review.

A free receptionist answers the call. It does not chase the offerte. It does not know rain is coming Thursday and the crew should be moved. It does not send the invoice, the reminder, or the review request that brings in the next three jobs.

So that is what I am building with Klantkraan: AI that runs a trade's whole customer workflow, from first message to paid invoice. The receptionist is just the front door.

Two commitments, on the record:

1. I build it in public. Real integrations, real failures, real numbers, every week, here.

2. Every assistant I ship tells customers it is AI. Not just because the EU AI Act requires it from August (it does), but because a tradesman's business runs on trust, and I will not spend his on a trick.

If you build vertical AI, or you run a trade business and know exactly which part of your admin you hate most, I want to hear from you. What I automate next is chosen by what bleeds money, not by what demos well.
