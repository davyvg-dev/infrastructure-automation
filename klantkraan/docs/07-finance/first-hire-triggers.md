# First-Hire Triggers

> The model breaks the moment the founder can't sustain 5-8 new clients/month while onboarding and supporting 30-60 active clients on 10-15 h/wk. Hire BEFORE the wall, not at it.

## Three planned hires

### Hire #1 — VA (virtual assistant)

|                       | Detail                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Trigger**           | 12 active clients (M5-6 base case) — NOT M8                                                                                |
| **Role**              | Onboarding ops, WhatsApp welcome QA, lead-notification monitoring, intake-extraction gap-filling (prices), weekly stats QA |
| **Hours**             | 30/mo to start (1.5h/day weekdays)                                                                                         |
| **Rate**              | €20/hr (NL VA market)                                                                                                      |
| **Cost**              | €600/mo                                                                                                                    |
| **Source candidates** | Hubstaff Talent, OnlineJobs.ph (PH-based at lower rate), Dutch native: Marktplaats / LinkedIn                              |
| **Onboarding**        | 2-week paid trial; founder records Loom-based SOPs for each task                                                           |
| **Reports to**        | Founder via WhatsApp + Linear                                                                                              |
| **Output**            | Onboarding-time per client drops from 130 min → 60-80 min founder time + 60 min VA time                                    |

A €600/mo VA saves ~1 churn save per quarter, which alone justifies the cost.

## Hiring vs. founder time math

Each €600 of monthly hire spend should free up ~10-15 founder hours/month (i.e., €40-60/h equivalent of opportunity cost). Pass that test before saying yes.

Quick gut-check: if a hire frees the founder for one additional discovery call per week → 4 calls/mo × 25% close × €299 ARPU = ~€300 NEW MRR / month from the hire's existence. A €600/mo hire pays back in ~2 months of compounding new MRR.

## Founder time budget by stage

| Month               | Active clients | Founder weekly hours          | Mix                                                          |
| ------------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| M1                  | 0-2            | ~25 h/wk (front-loaded build) | 80% product, 20% content                                     |

| M3                  | 5-8            | 12 h/wk                       | 40% delivery, 30% sales, 20% content, 10% ops                |
| M6 (pre-VA)         | 22             | 16 h/wk **over budget**       | 50% delivery, 20% sales, 20% content, 10% ops                |
| M6 (post-VA)        | 22             | 12 h/wk                       | 30% delivery, 30% sales, 25% content, 15% ops                |
| M9 (post-SDR)       | 39             | 12 h/wk                       | 20% delivery, 20% sales-management, 35% content, 25% ops     |
| M12 (post-engineer) | 60             | 12 h/wk                       | 15% delivery, 20% sales, 35% content/brand, 30% ops/strategy |

Founder time should ratchet DOWN on delivery and UP on content/strategy as hires absorb work.

## What "the wall" looks like

By M6 base case (22 active clients) without VA:

- Onboarding 5 new clients × 130 min = 10.8 h/mo just on onboarding
- Daily stats SMS QA + review-pipeline checks = 3 h/wk
- Discovery calls + offerte cycles = 6 h/wk
- Content production = 8 h/wk
- → ~21 h/wk needed before any strategy / planning / ops

That's above the 15h budget. Wall hit. VA averts it.
