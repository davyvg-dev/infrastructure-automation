# Dutch Leadgen Platforms — Competitive Intelligence for Klantkraan

**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Purpose:** Inform Klantkraan's positioning against incumbent lead platforms when selling to Dutch trade BVs (installateur, loodgieter, dakdekker, aannemer, schilder).

---

## Executive Summary (5 lines)

Werkspot is the dominant platform but vakmannen widely loathe its pay-per-lead model: a 20–30 % win rate means the real cost per acquired job runs ~€208 before labour starts. Homedeal and Advanza carry the same structural flaw (lead shared with 3–6 competitors, no refund on no-shows) plus mandatory subscription overhead on top. Slimster and Trustoo serve narrower niches and are less despised but still expensive relative to outcome. No platform on this list delivers owned, repeat relationships — the customer remembers the platform brand, not the vakman. Klantkraan's best angle: **owned-channel marketing automation that removes the per-lead tax forever** rather than yet another lead marketplace.

---

## Platform Deep-Dives

### 1. Werkspot

**Established:** 2005  
**Parent / legal entity:** Werkspot BV, KvK 18079951, Amsterdam (part of Instapro group)

**Business model**  
Pure pay-per-lead, no subscription required. Consumer posts a job for free; vakman browses and expresses interest; when consumer selects a vakman and contact details are exchanged, the fee is charged. Lead price: **€3–€75** depending on job type and scale — Adaptoo benchmarks it at €15–€45 for typical residential trades. Multiple vaks (typically 3–5) compete per lead simultaneously. No results guarantee; refunds only for invalid contact details, wrong location, duplicates, or instant rejects.

**Vetting**  
KvK SBI code matched to trade category. Valid ID required. For regulated trades (gas, CV, electrical, plumbing) a sector certification must be submitted and matched. Online skills test mandatory before first bids in painting, demolition, plumbing, general handyman. No insurance check disclosed; no BV-only filter (ZZP accepted). Geography: nationwide NL.

**Trustpilot (trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl)**  
~7,300 reviews. The bulk are consumer-side (positive: quick match, easy UI). Vakman-side reviews are a different story — see below.

**Vakman-side complaints (Radar forum, Klacht.nl, Higherlevel.nl, Wandgrimeurs blog — multiple threads 2022–2025)**

- Win rate disclosed by Adaptoo analysis: 20–30 %, meaning vakman must pay for 3–5 leads to convert one job.
- Effective cost per won job: ~**€208** (Adaptoo calculation).
- Lead price inconsistency: €28 charged on a €200 job; €8 on a €1,000 job — logic opaque and disputed by users.
- One ZZP forum user reported paying >€500 for contacts who never replied.
- Customer service reachable only by ticket; 48-hour response SLA poorly met per Radar threads.
- No brand stickiness: customers return to Werkspot for next job rather than calling the vakman directly.
- "Who bids fast and cheap wins" — race-to-bottom dynamic destroys margins for quality operators.

**Vakman-side praises**  
Volume of leads is high; useful for starting vaks who need pipeline quickly.

**Sentiment summary:** Structurally negative from vakman perspective. Positive only from consumer perspective and from new entrants before the economics become clear.

**BV vs consumer split**  
Primarily B2C (homeowner-driven). Jobs range from €100 tap repairs to €15,000 bathroom renovations. Mid-size residential projects (€500–€5,000) dominate. Not a B2B platform; not suited for commercial or infrastructure projects.

**Typical lead pricing range (Werkspot)**

- Small job (leaking tap, painting a room): €4–€15
- Mid job (bathroom renovation, CV install): €15–€40
- Specialist/regulated work: €40–€75

**Klantkraan recommendation:** PRIMARY COMPARISON FOIL. Every Klantkraan sales conversation with a trade BV starts here. The "I already pay Werkspot" objection is answered with the €208 effective acquisition cost and the no-repeat-customer problem.

### 2. Homedeal

**Established:** ~2010 (part of broader European home services network)  
**Scale:** 2,000+ registered professionals; 350,000+ jobs placed per year

**Business model**  
Pay-per-lead, no monthly subscription. Consumer submits a request form; platform matches up to 4 pre-screened professionals who each pay to unlock the contact details. Note: earlier Klussendirect analysis flagged a mandatory subscription on top of leads, but Homedeal's own FAQ (2024) states no subscription — may vary by contract tier. Lead price: typically **€40–€80** per lead per external sources; platform does not publish pricing publicly.

**Vetting**  
KvK registration required. Background screening, online reputation check, portfolio review, ongoing customer review monitoring. No specific insurance requirement disclosed. BV and ZZP both accepted. Nationwide NL.

**Trustpilot (trustpilot.com/review/homedeal.nl)**  
~493 reviews (NL). Predominantly consumer-side. Consumer satisfaction mixed — positives on quick response, negatives on contractors collecting deposits then ghosting (one user lost ~€550 to a near-bankrupt contractor; Homedeal expressed sympathy but took no action).

**Vakman-side complaints (Klussendirect comparison, Trustpilot vakman reviews)**

- Lead shared with up to 4 other professionals — high competition per job.
- Costs accumulate even when job does not proceed (consumer no-show, price shock, changed mind).
- No transparency on pricing before sign-up.
- Some users report receiving no jobs despite paying for access.
- Communication mediated through platform initially — reduces direct rapport.

**Vakman-side praises**  
Quality of homeowner requesting quotes is perceived as slightly higher than Werkspot (more intent, filtering by platform before match). Response time from interested homeowners reportedly quicker.

**Sentiment summary:** More neutral than Werkspot on the vakman side but fundamentally the same model critique applies. Thinner review volume (sample <500 on Trustpilot) limits confidence.

**BV vs consumer split**  
B2C. Similar job size profile to Werkspot: €500–€10,000 residential projects. Slightly higher-value job focus (kitchen/bathroom, extensions) versus Werkspot's broader small-jobs mix.

**Klantkraan recommendation:** COMPARISON FOIL alongside Werkspot. Mention in sales decks as "Werkspot with a different coat of paint."

### 3. Slimster

**Established:** ~2016  
**Focus:** Energy/sustainability projects (solar panels, insulation, heat pumps) plus broader home services

**Business model**  
Pay-per-lead. Consumer submits free request; max 3 professionals receive and pay for lead. No mandatory subscription. Lead price: **€15–€40** per lead (Klussendirect analysis).

**Vetting**  
Not publicly detailed. Standard KvK check implied; sustainability installer certification likely required for energy products.

**Feedback Company rating:** 8.7/10 from 1,022 reviews (primarily consumer-side).  
**Trustpilot (nl.trustpilot.com/review/slimster.nl):** present but lower volume than Werkspot.

**Vakman-side complaints (Klussendirect, Nederlandreview)**

- Lower lead volume than Werkspot or Homedeal — not viable as sole acquisition channel for general trades.
- Customers who complained post-sign-up report submitting contact data then facing spam/multiple follow-up calls from too many competitors.
- €15–€40 per lead with no conversion guarantee is still expensive if volume is thin.

**Vakman-side praises**  
Niche alignment: if you install heat pumps or solar, the lead quality is relatively high (homeowners who've done research, higher intent). Competition per lead capped at 3 (better than Werkspot's 5).

**Sentiment summary:** Niche-positive; poor fit for general trades like loodgieter or schilder.

**BV vs consumer split**  
B2C. Job values typically higher than general handyman (€3,000–€20,000 for heat pump installs, €5,000–€15,000 for solar). More relevant for installateurs with energy product line.

**Klantkraan recommendation:** NICHE IRRELEVANT for most Klantkraan target clients (schilder, loodgieter, dakdekker). For installateurs who do warmtepomp/solar — worth acknowledging as a channel but not a threat.

### 4. Trustoo (trustoo.nl)

**Established:** ~2018  
**Model:** Freemium marketplace guide — algorithm surfaces local Top 10 lists by specialty and location

**Business model**  
Basic profile free; premium per-lead model. Consumer views curated top-10 list and requests quote. Vakman pays **€20–€45 per lead** regardless of job outcome. Platform-mediated first contact. Nationwide NL; ~10,000 participating professionals.

**Vetting**  
KvK check; review-based scoring; ongoing satisfaction monitoring. No explicit insurance/BV requirement found.

**Trustpilot (trustpilot.com/review/trustoo.nl)**  
~3,910 reviews, 4 stars. Mix of consumer and vakman reviews.

**Vakman-side complaints (Klussendirect, Trustpilot vakman reviews)**

- "Betaal per contact, ook als klant niet reageert" — same structural flaw as all pay-per-lead platforms.
- One vakman: "I feel genuinely deceived and ripped off."
- Curated top-10 limits visibility for newer businesses with fewer reviews — self-reinforcing for established players.
- Platform-mediated initial contact reduces direct relationship.

**Vakman-side praises**  
Review-centric model encourages quality. High-intent consumer (browsing curated list rather than bulk-posting). Useful for premium contractors positioning on quality over price.

**Sentiment summary:** More positive than Werkspot/Homedeal from vakman side, but structurally identical lead-cost problem remains. Better suited to quality-positioned BVs than racing-to-bottom ZZPers.

**BV vs consumer split**  
B2C. Larger projects (renovation, extension, new build) dominate — lead quality above average. Job values €2,000–€50,000.

**Klantkraan recommendation:** MILD COMPETITOR in the "quality positioning" space. Klantkraan beats it on owned-channel (no per-lead cost) and on active outreach automation. Mention Trustoo in discovery if prospect values review platforms.

### 5. OfferteAdviseur (offerteadviseur.nl)

**Established:** 2010s  
**Model:** Quote comparison platform for consumers; lead marketplace for tradespeople

**Business model**  
Pay-per-lead, no subscription, no registration fees, no cancellation notice period. Shared, semi-shared, or exclusive lead tiers. Leads manually checked before dispatch. Max 3 recipients per lead (shared) or fewer (semi-shared/exclusive). Lead price indicative ~**€35** per shared lead based on one vakman review; exclusive leads higher. Google rating: 4.5/5 from 744 reviews.

**Vetting**  
Manual lead quality review before sending. KvK implied. Exclusive option limits competition. Phone support available (praised in reviews).

**Vakman-side feedback (Nederlandreview, Klussendirect, Google reviews)**

- Positives: phone-accessible customer support (rare in this space); pricing described as "one of the best in the Netherlands" by one reviewer; manual lead check reduces junk.
- Negatives: Some reviewers report low lead quality at €35/lead; overall volume lower than Werkspot.

**Sentiment summary:** Positively differentiated by phone support and manual quality filter. Still a pay-per-lead model but with better guardrails than Werkspot.

**BV vs consumer split**  
B2C. Residential renovations and services. Job range €500–€15,000.

**Klantkraan recommendation:** POTENTIAL PARTNER or INTEGRATION. OfferteAdviseur's lead quality focus aligns with Klantkraan's audience. Worth exploring whether overflow leads (e.g., jobs outside a BV's service radius) could feed into Klantkraan's automation workflows. Not a primary threat.

### 6. Advanza (advanza.nl — rebranded as Trustoo.nl frontend)

**Note:** Advanza's consumer-facing domain now redirects to Trustoo. Platform operates lead generation under the Trustoo brand in NL. Advanza continues as B2B lead platform across 100+ sectors.

**Business model**  
Lead shared with up to 6 businesses at **€10–€40 per lead** depending on sector. Active since 2009; 3,000+ SME partners.

**Vakman-side complaints (ZZP forum, Ervaringendelen, Nederlandreview 2022–2025)**

- Leads frequently unreachable or fake — customers claim they never signed up.
- Per-lead cost charged regardless; complaint process described as opaque and slow.
- Up to 6 businesses share one lead — highest competition ratio of any platform reviewed.
- One month cancellation notice required.

**Sentiment summary:** Most negative of all platforms reviewed from vakman perspective. Near-fraudulent lead quality complaints appear repeatedly across multiple independent sources.

**BV vs consumer split**  
Mix of B2C and B2B. But lead quality complaints make B2B viability questionable.

**Klantkraan recommendation:** AVOID and USE AS FOIL. Strongest negative signal in this research. Mention as "what can go wrong with lead platforms" in sales conversations.

### 7. Zoofy (zoofy.nl — owned by Centraal Beheer)

**Established:** ~2016; acquired by Centraal Beheer (insurer)  
**Model:** Commission/performance only — vakman pays a percentage of completed job value; no upfront lead fee.

**Business model**  
Consumer posts job free. Professionals see jobs in their area. Accept job → complete → Zoofy takes a percentage commission from earned revenue. No subscription, no per-lead charge. Partners with Gamma, Praxis, IKEA, Bol for job sourcing.

**Vetting**  
Screening process exists but criticized as "a sham" in Radar forum thread. Multiple complaints about unvetted contractors passing through.

**Vakman-side complaints (Radar, Klacht.nl 2023–2025)**

- Screening "is a wassen neus" (worthless) per Radar forum.
- Not phone-reachable; support is online-only.
- Commission model means margin pressure on small jobs.
- Some contractors describe arriving at jobs with no clarity on scope/cost.

**Vakman-side praises**  
No upfront cost is a major benefit for new or cash-constrained businesses. Partnership with large retailers (Gamma, IKEA) drives consistent volume.

**Sentiment summary:** Structurally better for cash-flow conscious vakmannen (commission vs. upfront lead cost). Quality concerns from both consumer and vakman sides.

**BV vs consumer split**  
B2C. Small to mid residential jobs (€100–€2,000). Not relevant for large project contractors.

**Klantkraan recommendation:** COMPARISON CONTEXT. Mention Zoofy to illustrate that even "fair" commission models have quality problems. Klantkraan's value proposition is that the vakman builds an owned brand, not platform dependency.

### 8. Bouwoffertes / Bouw-offertes.nl

**Scale:** Thin — delivers up to 6 quotes within 24 hours from regional firms.  
**Model:** Lead aggregator; consumer submits form, up to 6 local construction companies receive the request. Focus on larger residential construction and renovation (aannemer, dakdekker).

**Vetting:** Claims affiliated companies have "good track record." No public details on KvK/insurance process.

**Review data:** No independent Trustpilot profile found. Data too thin (<10 independent reviews located) to rate with confidence.

**Klantkraan recommendation:** PERIPHERAL. Small platform; include in exhaustive list for prospects but not a primary battleground.

## Cross-Platform Analysis

### BV vs Consumer Split

All platforms in this study are **primarily B2C** — homeowners and renters seeking quotes for residential projects. None explicitly require BV legal structure; ZZP is universally accepted. This is a positioning gap Klantkraan can exploit: trade BVs with structured operations (insurance, warranties, BV liability shield) get zero premium from any of these platforms over a ZZP sole trader. Klantkraan can articulate value specifically for the BV operator who wants leads that reflect their professional tier.

### Typical Job Value Ranges by Platform

| Platform        | Typical Consumer Job Range |
| --------------- | -------------------------- |
| Werkspot        | €100–€5,000                |
| Homedeal        | €500–€10,000               |
| Zoofy           | €100–€2,000                |
| Trustoo         | €2,000–€50,000             |
| Slimster        | €3,000–€20,000 (energy)    |
| OfferteAdviseur | €500–€15,000               |
| Advanza         | €200–€5,000                |

### Trade Willingness-to-Pay for a Warm Lead

Based on published and disclosed CPL data across platforms (Leadsprijzen.nl, Adaptoo, Klussendirect analysis, OfferteAdviseur reviews):

| Trade              | Disclosed CPL Range (market) | Notes                             |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Installateur/CV    | €15–€50                      | Higher for regulated gas/electric |
| Loodgieter         | €7–€40                       | Wide variance by job type         |
| Dakdekker          | €8–€25                       | Mid-range; high seasonal spike    |
| Aannemer (verbouw) | €12–€80                      | Large projects justify higher CPL |
| Schilder           | €9–€30                       | Competitive; thin margins         |
| Solar/warmtepomp   | €15–€35                      | Slimster/specialist platforms     |

Key insight: trades pay **€7–€80 per lead** depending on the trade and expected job value. At a 20–30 % win rate (Werkspot benchmark), the **effective cost per won job is €30–€400** before any labour cost. A dakdekker winning one in four leads at €25 each pays €100 to acquire a job; if that job is €800, the lead platform absorbs 12.5 % of revenue before a single tile is laid.

## Klantkraan Positioning Implications

1. **The lead-tax reframe works.** Every major platform charges €15–€80 per shared lead with 3–6 competitors. At a 25 % win rate, trades spend €60–€320 per acquired client. Klantkraan's productized marketing automation (Google/Meta/SEO, email follow-up, review generation) converts that spend into an **owned asset** — the next job from that client costs nothing.

2. **Platform dependency is the real pain.** No platform builds the vakman's brand. Werkspot customers call Werkspot again next time, not the schilder who did the job. Klantkraan promises brand ownership: "uw klanten worden uw vaste klanten."

3. **BV angle.** None of these platforms differentiate BV from ZZP. A Klantkraan prospect who runs a BV, has 4+ employees, and carries insurance is competing against a one-man ZZP on the exact same Werkspot listing. Klantkraan can pitch a dedicated presence that communicates the BV's professional level.

4. **Immediate sales objection response to "I already pay Werkspot":**  
   "How much did you pay Werkspot last month? And how many of those jobs became repeat customers who called you directly? Klantkraan turns that monthly lead spend into a self-filling pipeline — the platform can't take those clients back."
