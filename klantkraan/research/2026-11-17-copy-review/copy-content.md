### Flagged, not changed (legal pages)

1. **Legal form.** "T4 Software Consulting BV" on privacy (header, §1, §9) and voorwaarden (header, definitions). Open decision: it is an eenmanszaak. Related: voorwaarden "haar zakelijke opdrachtgevers", "Klantkraan of haar leidinggevenden" assume a BV.
2. **Scope of "Dienst".** The voorwaarden definition: "de productized marketing-automatisering ... tier (Chat of Compleet)". It covers only the receptionist tiers, not website care, automations, ad videos or the mini offers; "productized" is English inside a definition. For Davy and maybe a lawyer.
3. **Hosting location conflict.** DPA §5 says "Hetzner Frankfurt"; the subprocessors page says "Falkenstein DE, Helsinki FI"; the site review says Hetzner FSN1 (Falkenstein). At least one is wrong.
4. **"Opslag uitsluitend in de EU" (was "EU-only data residency")** in privacy §6, DPA §5 and Annex II, while the same documents list transfers to Anthropic, Twilio, Cloudflare and Resend (US). As written the claim contradicts the transfer sections. Wording kept; the claim needs checking.
5. **AI Act citations.** Privacy §4 and ai-disclosure §4 tie record-keeping and retention to "art. 50 lid 5". Art. 50(5) covers how and when the information is given (clear, at first interaction), not record-keeping. ai-disclosure §1 also says art. 50 lid 1 requires an opt-out to a human; art. 50(1) does not. The ai-disclosure page calls Klantkraan the "aanbieder (provider)"; whether Klantkraan or the client is the "gebruiksverantwoordelijke" (deployer) is a legal question. All of this needs a legal check.
6. **SLA status page.** §5 promises "een update op de statuspagina binnen 30 minuten" for S1, but §6 says the status page is not live yet.
7. **SLA "proefperiode" (was "trial").** The site offers no trial that I could find (/prijzen/ has "eerste maand 50% korting"). Check whether the clause is still needed.
8. **Subprocessor list may be incomplete.** The site's forms post to Brevo, but Brevo is not listed. Privacy §2 names Cloudflare Web Analytics, but no analytics script is in the source (Cloudflare Pages may inject it; please verify). Check whether Cal.com or other booking tools process data.
9. **Liability wording.** Voorwaarden §7 says "per geval" in one bullet and "per gebeurtenis" in the next, and the DPA says "per gebeurtenis". These could be read as two different units, so this is for a lawyer.
10. **"geen verdere remedie"** (voorwaarden §6, SLA §3): "remedie" is an anglicism of "remedy". The Dutch term ("rechtsmiddel", "aanspraak") could change the scope, so I left it.
11. **DPA §2 "Service Description".** No document by that name exists on the site; the voorwaarden point to /prijzen/ and the offerte.
12. **DPA §9 "De verwerker heeft een eigen DPIA per service-tier uitgevoerd"** and **§5 "Postgres TDE"** (Neon is managed Postgres): these are factual claims I could not verify.
13. **Voorwaarden §5 "Wet Incassokosten".** The official name is "Wet normering buitengerechtelijke incassokosten" (WIK), and its fixed scale is mandatory only for consumers. For a lawyer.
14. **Voorwaarden definition "het Service Level Agreement".** I left it because it is a definition; running text now says "de SLA" everywhere, matching §6 and sla.astro.
15. **Privacy §9 Contact** gives no postal address or KvK number. Only the email is shown, and it was listed twice before this edit.
