# Klantkraan Programmatic SEO Blueprint — /[stad]/[vak]

**Prepared:** 2026-05-21
**Scope:** 30 cities × 5 niches = 150 programmatic pages

---

## Executive Summary

Klantkraan has five active cornerstone pages (loodgieters, dakdekkers, installateur, aannemer, elektricien) and one schilder page is absent — this is gap to fill before Wave 1. SERPs for "[niche] [stad]" in major Dutch cities are dominated by small local business sites and light directories (Trustoo, Werkspot, Slimster), not by national brands with deep authority. This is the right market to attack with programmatic pages. Recommended launch order: Randstad G4 cities first, all five niches in parallel, with loodgieters and dakdekkers leading because cornerstone support is strongest. Wave 1 (10 pages) targets Amsterdam/Rotterdam/Den Haag/Utrecht/Eindhoven across loodgieters + dakdekkers. Thin-content risk is real at 150 pages — mitigate by templating at 600-900 words with mandatory city-specific data blocks, and by crawling in batches of 30 with a 6-week indexation window between waves.

## 2. Demand Signal Matrix — All 150 Combos

**Methodology:** These are proxy signals, not verified search volume numbers. Paid tools (Semrush, Ahrefs) were not available. Signals used:
- **Autosuggest presence:** whether "[niche] [stad]" appears in Google/Bing autocomplete (inferred from SERP competition count — more competing pages = query exists in autocomplete).
- **SERP competition:** number of dedicated city+niche domains observed in top-10 results, as a demand proxy (high competition = high volume).
- **City population tier:** larger cities have proportionally more search volume.
- **Economic fit:** whether the city's dominant sector drives above-average trade demand (e.g. port cities have more plumbing and roofing work than university towns per capita).

Buckets: **HIGH** = strong autosuggest signals, dedicated city-niche domains in top-10, large population + economic fit. **MED** = moderate signals, some dedicated domains, mid-size city. **LOW** = weak signals, no dedicated pages observed, smaller population.

## 5. Page Template Recommendations

Based on SERP analysis of ranking pages for "[niche] [stad]" queries.

### What currently ranks (pattern observed)

Most top-ranking local business pages are thin (200–500 words), have an H1 with "[Niche] [Stad]", a brief intro, a contact form or phone button, and sometimes a pricing table. Directories (Trustoo, Werkspot) have more depth: review summaries, filtering, FAQs. The opportunity is to be the most comprehensive, highest-trust result that is not a directory — filling the gap between thin local-business pages and aggregator directories.

### Required content blocks

| Block | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| H1: "[Niche] [Stad] — Klantkraan" | Exact-match H1 | E.g. "Loodgieters in Utrecht — Klantkraan" |
| Stad intro paragraph (100–150 words) | Localises the page; avoids thin-content penalty | Mention: population, known neighborhoods, housing stock type (canal houses, post-war estates, new-build), relevant local detail |
| Pain-stats block (city-adapted) | Social proof of the missed-call problem | Adapt the 28%/€450 stats from the cornerstone; add "In [stad] missen loodgieters gemiddeld..." framing |
| How Klantkraan works (3-block) | Product explanation | Identical to cornerstone; can be a component import |
| Lokale prijscontext | City-specific pricing signals | E.g. "Voorrijkosten in Amsterdam liggen doorgaans hoger (€75–120) dan in kleinere steden"; reference marktplaats/thuisbaas average quotes if available |
| Buurt/wijk sectie | Geographic long-tail depth | List 5–8 neighbourhoods/districts the niche operates in; internal anchor links to future sub-pages if warranted |
| FAQ (city-adapted, 4–6 vragen) | FAQPage schema + People Also Ask capture | At least 2 questions must reference the stad: "Hoe vind ik een loodgieter in Utrecht op korte termijn?", "Wat zijn gangbare uurtarieven voor loodgieters in Utrecht?" |
| Lokale referentie-blok | Trust signal | "Wij ondersteunen [niche]-bedrijven in [stad] en de regio [X]" + list known districts covered |
| CTA band | Conversion | Same as cornerstone: demo + ROI calculator |
| Internal links | Link equity flow | See below |

### Schema types required

- `LocalBusiness` (type: `Plumber` / `RoofingContractor` / `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` as appropriate) with `areaServed` set to the city name and `@id` pointing to the cornerstone page
- `FAQPage` with `Question`/`Answer` pairs from the FAQ block
- `BreadcrumbList`: Home > [Niche cornerstone] > [Stad page]
- Optional: `Service` schema for the AI-receptionist service itself

Do NOT use `LocalBusiness` to describe Klantkraan as if it is the tradesperson. Use `SoftwareApplication` or `Service` for Klantkraan itself, and a separate `LocalBusiness` wrapper only for the described trade in the `areaServed` context.

### Internal linking pattern

```
/loodgieters/  (cornerstone)
  └── /amsterdam/loodgieter
  └── /rotterdam/loodgieter
  └── /utrecht/loodgieter
        └── links back to /loodgieters/
        └── links laterally to /amsterdam/loodgieter, /rotterdam/loodgieter (nearby cities)
        └── links to /utrecht/dakdekker (same-city cross-niche)
```

Rules:
1. Every city+niche page links UP to its niche cornerstone (e.g. /loodgieters/).
2. Every city+niche page links LATERALLY to 2–3 neighbouring city pages of the same niche.
3. Every city+niche page links CROSS to 1–2 other niches in the same city (e.g. Utrecht/loodgieter links to Utrecht/dakdekker).
4. Cornerstones link DOWN to city pages in a "Wij werken ook in [steden]:" section — add this section to cornerstones progressively as cities go live.
5. Neighboring cities to link: Amsterdam ↔ Haarlem, Haarlemmermeer, Almere, Zaanstad. Rotterdam ↔ Dordrecht, Delft, Den Haag. Utrecht ↔ Amersfoort, Nieuwegein (not on list), Almere. Eindhoven ↔ Tilburg, Den Bosch, Breda.

### Word count target

**600–900 words** per page. This is enough to satisfy Google's thin-content detector for programmatic pages while remaining practical to template. Do not pad with generic SEO boilerplate. Every sentence must be either city-specific, product-specific, or a genuine FAQ answer. The existing cornerstone loodgieters.astro is approximately 800 words of visible copy — use this as the length ceiling, with ~200–300 words of unique city content replacing the generic product intro.

### URL structure

```
/[stad]/[vak]
```

Where `[stad]` = lowercase, hyphenated city name (den-haag, den-bosch, s-hertogenbosch → use den-bosch for simplicity, add canonical if needed), and `[vak]` = singular noun (loodgieter, dakdekker, installateur, aannemer, schilder).

Examples:
- `/amsterdam/loodgieter`
- `/den-haag/schilder`
- `/s-hertogenbosch/aannemer` (or `/den-bosch/aannemer` — pick one, add redirect for the other)

Do not use `/niche/stad` — the city-first structure is preferred because it groups by geography, enabling a future `/amsterdam/` hub page summarising all five niches in the city.

**Astro implementation:** Use a `src/pages/[stad]/[vak].astro` dynamic route with a `getStaticPaths()` function, or a data-driven approach with a `cities.ts` data file. Recommended: data-driven. Each city entry in the data file carries: `slug`, `name`, `province`, `population`, `housingStock` (brief descriptor), `neighbourhoods` (array of 6–8), `priceTier` (hoog/gemiddeld/laag), `intro` (1 sentence of unique local colour). The template consumes this at build time. This approach produces genuinely differentiated pages rather than pure mass-duplication.

## 6. Crawl Budget and Indexation Strategy

### Why 150 pages is a risk

Klantkraan is a new domain. Google allocates crawl budget proportional to perceived authority. Dumping 150 thin-to-medium pages simultaneously signals low quality — and if Google perceives them as a content farm, it may sandbox the domain or apply a sitewide thin-content action.

### Staged rollout plan

| Stage | Pages live | Timing | Trigger to proceed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cornerstones | 5 existing + /schilder/ = 6 | Already live / add before Wave 1 | Already indexed (check GSC) |
| Wave 1 | +10 (top G4 × 2 niches) | Launch date | All 10 submitted to GSC; wait until 8+ indexed |
| Wave 2 | +10 | T + 6 weeks | Google Search Console shows Wave 1 pages receiving impressions (even 0-click) |
| Wave 3 | +10 | T + 12 weeks | Wave 2 indexed and no GSC coverage issues (Excluded > Crawled not indexed < 20%) |
| Waves 4–10 | Remaining 120 in batches of 10–20 | T + 18 weeks onward | One batch per 3–4 weeks |

At this cadence, all 150 pages are live by approximately T + 40 weeks (~10 months). This is conservative but safe for a new domain.

### Thin-content prevention

1. **City-unique paragraph mandatory.** At least one paragraph (100+ words) must be genuinely city-specific: reference local housing types, known districts, or a local market fact. Templated filler ("Amsterdam is een geweldige stad") does not qualify.
2. **FAQ must contain city name.** At least 2 of 4–6 FAQ items must include the stad name in the question text.
3. **No duplicate meta descriptions.** Each page gets a unique `description` generated from the city data file. Pattern: "[Vak] in [Stad]? Klantkraan helpt [vak]s in [stad] gemiste oproepen terugbellen, reviews te verzamelen en klanten te boeien — per maand opzegbaar."
4. **Avoid auto-generated on-page headings** that make city swapping obvious ("Loodgieter Amsterdam — Loodgieter Rotterdam" are structurally identical). Vary H2 subheadings per city cluster (Randstad, Brabant, Noord, Oost).
5. **Internal link density.** 3–5 internal links per page (see section 5). Google uses internal links as a quality signal for programmatic pages.

### Sitemap strategy

Use Astro's built-in sitemap plugin (already configured). Extend it to include `lastmod` and `changefreq` per page. City pages should have:
- `changefreq: monthly`
- `priority: 0.6` (lower than cornerstones at 0.8)

Do not submit all 150 to GSC at once. Use the `URL Inspection` tool in GSC to manually request indexation of Wave 1 (10 pages) and Wave 2 (10 pages). For Waves 3+, rely on organic crawl — the sitemap will surface them.

### robots.txt

No blocking needed. Ensure the future city route is NOT blocked under any current robots.txt directive. Check `/robots.txt` currently reads clean (it does: public/robots.txt allows all).

### Duplicate-content risk from neighbouring cities

Cities near each other (Leiden and Delft are 15 km apart, both small, both university towns) will produce structurally similar pages. Mitigate by:
- Using `canonical` to point self-referential canonicals on each page (no cross-canonical between cities).
- Writing distinct neighbourhood lists and distinct local intro paragraphs.
- Ensuring the pricing context block uses different data (Leiden tends to have higher contractor rates than Delft due to biotech demand; Delft's TU proximity affects HVAC).
