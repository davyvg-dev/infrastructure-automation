# Track 3 — Market & pricing research: €1.000 fixed-price website as Klantkraan add-on

## TL;DR verdict

**€1.000 ex BTW is a credible, defensible price — but only with a recurring fee attached (€29–49/mo hosting+onderhoud) and only if positioned as "spoedklus-website binnen een week, jij blijft eigenaar", not as generic webdesign.** €1.000 sits exactly in the gap between the budget-productized cluster (€175–€749) and agency floor (€1.500–€3.000). The bigger finding: **the website+AI-telefonist bundle already exists in NL** — STUDIOLEE and LoodgieterAI sell exactly this to loodgieters today, at €79/mo entry points. Klantkraan is not first; it must out-position, not just show up.

## 2. Platform competitors & adoption stats

- DIY builders trades actually use: [JouwWeb from €6–8,50/mo](https://www.jouwweb.nl/tarieven), Webador, Wix from ~$17/mo. All sub-€20/mo — the real price anchor in a tradesman's head.
- **CBS ([ICT-gebruik bij bedrijven](https://longreads.cbs.nl/ict-kennis-en-economie-2022/ict-gebruik-bij-bedrijven/)): only 76–77% of companies with 2+ employees have a website; only ~34% of ZZP'ers do.** Caveat: 2022 data, not trades-specific.
- Werkspot as website substitute: leads cost €3–75 each; per [Adaptoo](https://adaptoo.nl/blog/werkspot-kosten) a tradesman pays **~€208 in lead fees per won job**. Strongest economic ammunition for "own your own channel."

## 3. What does €1.000 buy in NL today?

~€1.000 buys a 5–6 page site with basic design and stock photos from a junior freelancer (rates €50–75/h; experienced €75–120/h; agencies €100–150/h per [Stuurlui](https://stuurlui.nl/kennis/wat-vraagt-een-webdesigner-in-nederland-per-uur/)). **€1.000 is the top of "budget" and the floor of "professional" — honest-mid.** Agencies charge 1,5–3x for comparable. Goedgestart/STUDIOLEE-type offers visibly undercut it in Google results.

## 4. Recurring economics

- NL maintenance market: **€25–50/mo simple sites, €50–150/mo serious MKB** ([webwrk](https://www.webwrk.nl/blog/website-onderhoud-kosten), [Brightbrands](https://brightbrands.online/website/website-onderhoud-kosten/), [GraphicGenie](https://www.graphicgenie.nl/blog/wat-kost-website-onderhoud-per-maand)). Cost basis: €5–10/mo hosting; .nl domain €10–20/yr. €39/mo on a static/Astro site is ~90% margin.
- Churn (global, no NL data): retainer relationships churn ~18%/yr vs ~42% project work; lifespan ~56 months on retainers ([WinSavvy](https://www.winsavvy.com/productized-service-business-models-revenue-churn-benchmarks/), [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-marketing-agency-churn/)). Two subscriptions cancel less readily than one.
- Standard upsell ladder: SEO (€299 one-off at Goedgestart), Google Ads mgmt, review mgmt, chatbot — STUDIOLEE runs this exact ladder.

## 5. Willingness to pay (Dutch trades)

- Small plumbing company advised €200–500/mo total marketing budget ([Searchlab](https://searchlab.nl/kosten/hoeveel-marketingbudget-heb-je-nodig-als-mkb); NL rule of thumb 6–8% of B2B revenue). Agency-blog numbers, not survey data.
- Real observed spend: Werkspot fees (~€208/won job), Google Ads for spoed keywords (expensive, fraud-ridden — [Consumentenbond](https://www.consumentenbond.nl/verbouwen-en-klussen/een-goede-loodgieter-vinden) and [MAX Meldpunt](https://www.maxmeldpunt.nl/oplichting/snel-loodgieter-nodig-pas-op-voor-oplichters/) document scam-plumber ads dominating spoed searches — trust angle a legitimate local site can exploit).
- How they buy: word of mouth first, then "loodgieter [plaats]" on Google; callers pick first credible result ([InternetYou](https://internetyou.nl/blog/online-marketing-voor-vakmensen)). No Techniek Nederland channel data found.
- €1.000 one-off + €39/mo ≈ 2–3 months of acceptable marketing budget. Affordable, but sell the _klus_, not the site.

## 6. Bundle logic — the wedge already exists, both directions

- **NL competitors already bundling:** [STUDIOLEE](https://www.studiolee.nl/voice-ai) sells loodgieter-websites from €79/mo AND AI Telefonist from €79/mo + €0,25–0,80/min. [LoodgieterAI](https://www.loodgieterai.nl/) sells all-in tiers €79/€297/€497/mo incl. website + voice AI + chatbot + reviews. Also: [InstallatieTelefoniste.nl](https://www.installatietelefoniste.nl/), [Voicelabs](https://www.voicelabs.nl/voor-loodgieters), [Beller.io](https://www.beller.io/), aiagent.nl. Klantkraan's €299–499/mo is _above_ these entry points — the website add-on justifies the premium, but expect €79/mo price objections.
- **US validation:** [Housecall Pro includes a website builder](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/websites/) (free first year, even for non-customers) as top-of-funnel wedge; Jobber lacks it and gets beaten on this. [Dialzara](https://dialzara.com/) bundles a website chatbot free with voice plans. "Missed-call → booked job" funnel with a site as front door is a proven motion.

## 7. Legal / commercial (NL)

- **IP:** default under auteursrecht the _builder_ keeps copyright; transfer requires written akte — put explicit IP transfer on final payment ([ICTRecht](https://www.ictrecht.nl/blog/wie-is-eigenaar-van-een-in-opdracht-gemaakte-website), [LAWFOX](https://lawfox.nl/intellectueel-eigendom-website/)). Competitors advertise "jij blijft eigenaar" — Klantkraan should too.
- **Domain:** register in the **client's** name from day one ([ICTRecht](https://www.ictrecht.nl/blog/wie-is-de-houder-van-een-domeinnaam), [BG.legal](https://bg.legal/wie-heeft-recht-op-de-domeinnaam/)).
- **BTW:** design + hosting/maintenance both 21% ([Holdwise](https://holdwise.nl/kennisbank/btw-tarieven-uitleg)); one-off + subscription on one invoice fine — same NET-grossed-up-at-Mollie approach. NOTE: existing repo blocker — seller BTW-id/address placeholders must be fixed before invoices are legally valid.

## 8. Pricing verdict

**Keep €1.000 — as the anchor, not the only number.**

- Below €1.000 → head-on with Goedgestart (€549) and €79/mo WaaS on their terms, cheapens the receptionist. Above €1.500 → agency territory, comparison shopping.
- Recommended structure:
  1. **Standalone: €1.000 ex BTW + verplicht €39/mo hosting & onderhoud** (monthly cancellable after 6 mo). €39/mo is the real product — near-pure margin, retention anchor, inside NL €25–50 norm.
  2. **Bundled with receptionist: €749** ("€1.000, nu €749 bij Compleet") — expiring reason to sign now, mirrors founding-offer mechanic.
  3. **Do NOT give the site away free** — free signals template-spam in a market burned by scam loodgieter-ads.
- Skeptical note on "1 hour": competitors deliver in 6–14 _days_ because content collection (photos, service areas, KvK/reviews, tone) is the bottleneck, not the build. Quote "binnen een week online" publicly even if the build is an hour; budget 2–4 hours real founder time per site for intake and revisions.

## 9. Three strongest go-to-market angles

1. **"Stop met huren van je klanten" — Werkspot-independence pitch.** ~€208 lead fees per won job vs €1.000 once + €39/mo. Five won jobs pays for the site. Math-first; no €79/mo bundler leads with this.
2. **Complete missed-call funnel sold as one outcome.** "Klant zoekt 'loodgieter + spoed' → vindt uw site → belt → Klantkraan neemt op, plant in, stuurt bevestiging — ook om 23:00." Site alone and receptionist alone are both commodities now; the _guaranteed handled spoedklus_ is the product. The prospect demo site literally becomes the deliverable.
3. **"Binnen een week online, vaste prijs, en het is van ú"** — target the no-website majority (2/3 of ZZP'ers, 1/4 of small firms). Lead with the three fears: cost surprises (vaste prijs), duration (één week), lock-in (domein op uw naam, IP-overdracht op papier — unlike WaaS where cancelling means losing the site).
