Six parallel research tracks (pain quantification, vertical ranking, channels, competitors, list-building signals, internal audit), synthesized. Full sources inline. Goal: one provable pain point, one vertical, one next step.

## Executive summary

1. **The bottleneck is dials, not assets.** The repo holds 30 branded demos, a 259-line cold-call playbook, a 193-line call sheet and 25 pipeline prospects — and exactly **one logged phone call, ever**. The email sequence is stalled (11 prospects with touch 2 overdue 7–9 days, 6 never got touch 1, zero replies across ~30 sends). No research finding matters more than this.
2. **The pain point to lead with is speed-to-answer, made provable per prospect** — not industry statistics. Mystery-call the prospect before pitching; mine their Google reviews for "neemt niet op". Their own missed call beats any percentage.
3. **New market fact that sharpens the pitch:** Google **banned search ads** for slotenmakers (2021) and loodgieters — explicitly including ontstoppingsdiensten — in NL since 22-02-2024. These trades cannot buy a missed lead back at any price. Every answered call is irreplaceable revenue.
4. **The "only trades player" window has closed.** 15+ Dutch AI-telefonist players are live, including two direct niche clones (InstallatieTelefoniste €0,25/min; LoodgieterAI €79/mo all-in). Free 7–30-day trials are the market norm. Remaining edges: text-first, real booking depth (RDW/PDOK/agenda), per-prospect branded demos, owner-in-the-loop takeover. Speed matters now.
5. **First-vertical recommendation: rioolontstopping**, with slotenmakers as the active pipeline (close DRS) and garages as the parallel cold-email track (DHZ demo + the only NL-specific reachability stat: 71% of 4.500+ garages failed phone checks).

## 1. The pain point, made sellable

**Mechanism (undisputed, no stats needed):** a caller with a verstopt toilet or a lockout dials down the Google results until someone answers. Voicemail = the job goes to the next listing. For ad-banned spoed trades the lead is gone forever.

**Stats cleared for use (defensible):**
- 78% of customers buy from the company that responds first — MIT/InsideSales via HBR, the only academically grounded number in this space.
- "Bijna de helft van de terugbelbeloftes wordt nooit nagekomen" — KCM Group NL mystery-call research (10.000 calls), via DIRECT Klantcontact. This is what a message-taking antwoordservice *produces*.
- 1 op 12 bellers krijgt helemaal geen contact (same KCM research; all-business average — small trades are worse).
- 71% van 4.500+ autobedrijven telefonisch onbereikbaar bij één of meer checks (Places.nl; **verify in a browser before print** — JS page, methodology unconfirmed).
- ~27% of home-services inbound calls missed (Invoca, US 60M-call data; label as international).

**Stats banned from copy (vendor folklore every competitor parrots):** "62% unanswered" (traces to a 2016 study of n=85), "85% never calls back", "20% spreekt voicemail in", "belt 3 nummers in 5 minuten". Unsourced or ancient. Credibility-first positioning cannot afford them.

**The euro math (NL-sourced job values):**
| Vertical | Spoed job value | One missed job/week costs |
|---|---|---|
| Rioolontstopping | €90–250, met camera €270–480, spoedtoeslag +60–100% | €600–1.500/mo |
| Slotenmaker | €80–150 opening, avond/nacht €100–150+ alleen al | €600–1.000/mo |
| Loodgieter spoed | €150–400 per klus | €600–1.600/mo |
| Garage | €200–600 per werkplaatsbezoek | (afspraak-gedreven, lagere urgentie) |

Framing rule: state the formula ("X gemiste calls × €Y per klus"), let the prospect fill in X. Against €299–499/mo the math closes at 1–2 saved jobs.

**The hardest proof costs nothing:** call the prospect 2–3× during a workday before pitching. "Ik belde u dinsdag om 10:15 en 14:40 — geen gehoor. Dat was ik. Meestal is het een klant met een lekkage."

**Structural driver for the objection "neem toch iemand aan":** 67–68% of technical firms report staff shortage; 75.600 open technical vacancies end-2024; 121.000 vakmensen needed in 5 years (Techniek Nederland). A telefoniste is not hireable at this wage level.

## 3. Competitive reality check (aug 2026)

Market moved from "nearly empty" (July research) to crowded:

- **Direct niche clones:** InstallatieTelefoniste (Flireo B.V.) — installateurs/loodgieters only, €0,25/min, geen abonnement, setup v.a. €250, live in 3 days, browser demo. LoodgieterAI — €79/mo all-in (voice+chatbot+CRM), 14-day trial €1.
- **Serious generalists naming trades:** Voicelabs "Robin" (€299/500 min → €899, live demo number, ISO 27001, dedicated loodgieters page), VoxFlow (v.a. €99), LokaleAI (€125–195, **"pay only after you approve go-live"**), Growguide (€349–497).
- **Mass market entering:** Places.nl "AI Daan" (30 days free), Strato, MIKE365 (€14,99 entry). Antwoordservice Nederland now hybrid-AI via Bonnie (AI handles ~75%, human takes over).
- **No US invasion found** (Slang.ai/Goodcall/Curious Thing not in NL); the pressure is bottom-up Vapi/Retell white-labelers.

**Positioning implications:**
- Art. 50 compliance is **in force for everyone since 02-08-2026** (NOS/Ziptone) → reframe from "the compliant one" to "compliant by design from day one" as trust signal.
- Text-first + booking-depth + branded per-prospect demos remain distinctive; self-serve demos alone are now table stakes.
- €499 voice/750 min: defensible mid-market (between Voicelabs Start and Compleet). Install fee €945–1.395 is top-of-market — justify with bespoke triage or expect pushback. €299 chat is the exposed flank (Voicelabs sells *voice* at that price) — sell it as "receptionist die klussen inboekt", never "chatbot".
- €149 first month is **no longer aggressive de-risking** — free trials are the norm. Strongest close in market: LokaleAI's "pay only when you approve go-live". Consider matching for the first client(s); Voicelabs' own market report validates the narrow-pilot approach (start small, one use case, validate).
- Voicelabs' market report predicts price compression 2026-27 → LiveKit-EU margin migration matters more, not less.

## 4. Channel plan (ranked by P(first client ≤30d) × effort)

Every traceable comparable (Smith.ai, Goodcall, Slang.ai, Bizziphone, fonio.ai) got first customers via **founder-led direct sales in one tight vertical + a partner who already bills the SMB** — never content/community/brand.

1. **Cold call with pre-built evidence ("gemiste-oproep audit")** — 10–20% of qualified conversations → appointment (NL telesales benchmarks); phone ≈ 5× email for this segment. Best windows for owner-operators: di–do, 7:45–9:00 en 16:30–17:45. Script: audit-opener → WhatsApp the branded demo link mid-call ("mag ik u de link appen?") → close on concrete start day.
2. **Cold email to B.V.'s as air support** (38–52% opens in trades, ~3–5% reply) — never standalone; known deliverability problem (DRS mails hit spam; DMARC still p=none — fix before scaling).
3. **Agency referral wedge (30–90d payoff):** marketing agencies serving installateurs/garages — Marketingoo, Cherry Marketing, Noviqum, Peakfort, AdsVIBE, Hey Harry, Market-Ai. Pitch: "uw campagnes leveren belletjes die niet worden opgenomen; wij vangen ze — uw ROI-rapportage wordt beter. 20% recurring of white-label. Eén gratis client-slot om het te bewijzen." Smith.ai built its growth on exactly this program.
4. **VoIP partner channel (6-mo horizon, start now):** Rinkel co-markets Bizziphone (exact template); Voys has a formal reseller program; Belfabriek is building a "telefonie & AI-voicebots" partner channel (window closing). Send partner applications this month.
5. **Vakbeurzen as visitor:** Installatie Vakdagen Hardenberg **8–10 sept** (bullseye audience, 4 weeks out), Vakbeurs Energie Den Bosch 6–8 okt, BOVAG Supercharge 6 okt. Demo-QR on the phone; dozens of owner conversations for a ticket price.
6. Skip for now: BNI (€1,8k/yr, months-to-payoff), Facebook groups (job boards, not owner rooms), brancheorganisatie partnerships (pay-to-play, slow), LinkedIn (owners barely present).

## 5. List building: the signal pipeline (one afternoon per city)

Verified live today (Utrecht/Rotterdam SERPs + Ads Transparency Center):

1. **Maps review mining (60–90 min) — primary discovery.** Maps → top ~15 profiles per trade per city → "zoeken in reviews": `neemt niet op`, `bereikbaar`, `voicemail`, `teruggebeld`, `geen reactie`. +3 per recent complaint. Golden combo: profiel zegt "24 uur geopend" én review zegt "niet bereikbaar".
2. **Evening SERP harvest by hand (20 min, ~19:00–21:00, on the phone) — secondary.** Google spoed keywords per city, note "Gesponsord" advertisers, verify each at adstransparency.google.com (region=NL) — which also shows the **verified legal entity incl. rechtsvorm** (free BV check). Caveat discovered live: the Transparency Center is **not keyword-searchable** (verification only), Google serves no ads to automated sessions (must be done by hand), and **most top spoed-SERP domains are SEO lead-farms with 0 ads** (Veenstra-network, mrloodgieter*, de247spoedloodgieter, loodgieterscollectief, slotenmaker-expert) — national footprint + 085-nummer + interchangeable city pages = not a prospect. Real firms: local kengetal, one city, "15+ jaar actief".
3. **Mystery call the top ~10 (30 min, after 18:00 or Saturday) — verification + opener.** One call, don't record, no fake emergency. No answer/voicemail = +3, pain confirmed first-party. Normalized practice in NL (excap, klantgemak).
4. **BV filter/coverage:** free per-company on kvk.nl; bulk via **Ad Hoc Data** (adhocdata.nl) — KvK-based, €0,07–0,08/record + €5, instant CSV, SBI × regio × rechtsvorm. **This unblocks the list machine without the KvK API key.** (KVK's own offline selectie: €334 setup + ~2 weeks. Free KVK Open Dataset is useless: no names/addresses/KvK-numbers.)
5. Score ≥5 = A-list; opener writes itself from the signal found.

**Sample candidates from today's live run (unverified unless noted):** RRS Nederland BV (rrs.nl, Utrecht, riool — confirmed active advertiser ~400 ads, Verified BV; possibly too large), Ontstoppingsbedrijf PROF (Utrecht — sluit om 22:00, prime missed-call candidate), Stichtse Riool Reiniging, Ontstoppingsdienst ORS, Van Elburg Riooltechniek (Utrecht/Zeist), U-Sloten (855 reviews, "24 uur geopend"), Slotenmaker Guus Offerbeek, Van Dijk, Van Kessel (Utrecht), Rotterdamse Loodgieter, Loodgieterstad, Loodgieter Erasmus, M&H (Rotterdam).
