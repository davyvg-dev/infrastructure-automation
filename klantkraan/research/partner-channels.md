# Klantkraan — Partner Channel Research
**Date:** 2026-05-21 | **Scope:** NL trade BVs (installateur, loodgieter, dakdekker, aannemer, schilder)

## Exec Summary

Five warm-channel categories warrant immediate pursuit. Branch organizations hold the largest concentrated reach (6,000–4,600 members per org) and have documented partner intake pathways. Accounting software vendors (Exact, Visma eAccounting) and field-service apps (OutSmart, Simple-Simon) already sit inside the daily workflow of trade BVs and either run documented reseller programs or have a kickback-fee precedent. Trade events in 2026-2027 skew toward installer and contractor profiles, with VSK+E (Feb 2026) and Installatie Vakdagen Hardenberg (Sep 2026) being the highest-density ICP venues. Priority sequence: (1) Techniek Nederland branchepartner, (2) Bouwend Nederland voordeelpartner, (3) OutSmart/Simple-Simon co-marketing, (4) Visma eAccounting accountant partner network, (5) OnderhoudNL partner program.

## 1. Branch Organizations (Branche-organisaties)

### Techniek Nederland
- **Status:** Active. Confirmed via technieknederland.nl (visited 2026-05-21).
- **Background:** Renamed from UNETO-VNI; Techniek Nederland is the correct current name. FME is a separate federation for manufacturing; not merged.
- **Membership:** 6,000+ affiliated companies in installation (mechanical, electrical, sanitary) and electrotechnical retail. Total sector: 46,000+ businesses, €35.8B turnover, 194,000 employees.
- **Member profile:** Primarily SME installation contractors. Mix of BV and eenmanszaak; no public BV/ZZP split disclosed.
- **Partner program (Branchepartner):** Formally documented. Open to organizations not focused on installation/maintenance themselves (so software/marketing providers qualify). Two tiers: Base (up to 10 employees) and Plus (10+ employees). Benefits include logo use, network access, magazine (E&W), advisory services, and insurance discounts. Application via branchepartner@technieknederland.nl or 088 5432 790. No public cost listed — contact required.
- **Contact for partnerships:** branchepartner@technieknederland.nl | 088 543 2790 (ledenservice)
- **ICP overlap:** High. Core members are exactly Klantkraan's ICP.

### OnderhoudNL
- **Status:** Active. Confirmed via onderhoudnl.nl (visited 2026-05-21).
- **Membership:** 2,200+ affiliated businesses (schilders, glaszetters, vastgoedonderhoud, isolatiebedrijven). 30,000 people working across the sector.
- **Member profile:** Mix of SMEs and ZZP. Organization explicitly courts MKB and ZZP schilders. BV penetration is moderate — schilder/glazier sector skews smaller than installatie. Relevant for Klantkraan's schilder ICP segment.
- **Partner program:** OnderhoudNL Partners is a documented program. Partners are selected based on member demand/need. Current partners include Wildkamp (technical wholesaler) and Vereniging Eigen Huis. No public revenue-share or fee structure.
- **Contact:** info@onderhoudnl.nl | 088-0188 188 | Tielweg 16, 2803 PK Gouda
- **ICP overlap:** Medium-high for schilder segment; lower for installateur.

### Bouwend Nederland
- **Status:** Active. Confirmed via bouwendnederland.nl (visited 2026-05-21).
- **Membership:** ~4,600 construction and infrastructure companies. Largest contractor employer organization in NL.
- **Member profile:** Aannemers, bouwbedrijven, infrabedrijven. Heavy on BV structures given scale requirements. Overlaps with Klantkraan's aannemer ICP.
- **Partner program (three tiers):**
  - *Voordeelpartner*: Commercial party offering member discounts/benefits. Must demonstrate added financial value. Serves 4,600+ members nationwide.
  - *Netwerkpartner* (Informatielid): For service providers (accountants, software suppliers) who cannot join as regular members. Annual fee €875–€2,850 depending on company revenue. Grants portal access, magazines, newsletter, and first-line advisory.
  - *Strategische partner*: Minimum 3-year commitment, exclusivity on a focus theme. Knowledge-sharing oriented.
- **Contact for partnerships:** Roeland Contzé (manager partners) | voordeel@bouwendnederland.nl | 079-3252166
- **ICP overlap:** High for aannemer segment.

### AFNL — Aannemersfederatie Nederland Bouw en Infra
- **Status:** Active. Confirmed via aannemersfederatie.nl (visited 2026-05-21).
- **Structure:** Federation of 12 member branch organizations (MKB Bouw, VSB, Gebouwschil Nederland, Straatwerk, Isolerend Nederland, NBVT, Cumela, others). Not a direct company membership org.
- **Membership numbers:** Not publicly disclosed (indirect reach via member orgs).
- **Publication:** BouwBelang magazine, 4x/year, print run 10,000 copies + monthly digital newsletter. Sponsor/advertiser channel worth investigating.
- **Contact:** secretariaat@aannemersfederatie.nl | 0318-54 49 00 | Director: Jaco Uittenbogaard (uittenbogaard@aannemersfederatie.nl)
- **ICP overlap:** Medium. Reach is through federations; less direct than Bouwend Nederland.
- **Note:** No documented external partner program. Advertising/media sponsorship in BouwBelang is the practical entry point.

### NVKL — Nederlandse Vereniging Koudetechniek & Luchtbehandeling
- **Status:** Active. Confirmed via nvkl.nl (visited 2026-05-21).
- **Membership:** 450+ companies across the supply chain (installation, suppliers, consultancies, education).
- **Member profile:** Refrigeration and HVAC installers, climate control specialists. Typically SME, BV-heavy due to F-gas certification and equipment investment requirements.
- **Partner program:** No documented external partner program found. Member discount programs exist (e.g., 10% off Techniek Carrièrebeurs).
- **Contact:** Via helpdesk at nvkl.nl/over-nvkl/contact/
- **ICP overlap:** Medium-high (specialist HVAC installers are a Klantkraan sub-segment).
- **Note:** Smaller association; lower leverage than Techniek Nederland.

### VLOK — Branchevereniging voor vakmensen (klussenbedrijven)
- **Status:** Active. Confirmed via vlok.nl (visited 2026-05-21).
- **Background:** VLOK stands for Vereniging van Klusbedrijven; it is NOT the loodgieters/koper/zink trade org as originally assumed — it is the association of maintenance/klusbedrijven (handymen and mixed-trade firms). The loodgieter/copper/zinc sector is primarily covered under Techniek Nederland (W-installateurs).
- **Membership:** Size not publicly disclosed. Partners include Wildkamp (water technology wholesaler) and Bouwmaat (purchasing discounts).
- **Partner program:** Partners are accepted; current partners include technical wholesalers. No formal public application process found — contact required.
- **ICP overlap:** Lower than Techniek Nederland. VLOK members skew smaller/mixed-trade, many ZZP.

### Other relevant organizations not in the original list
- **FME** (federatie metaalunie voor technologische industrie) — manufacturing sector, not installation. Not relevant.
- **MKB Bouw** (via AFNL) — SME contractor association; reach ~5,000 small building contractors; access point for aannemer ICP.
- **Stroomversnelling** — coalition for energy-renovation (warmtepompen, isolatie); member installers overlap strongly with Klantkraan ICP. Documented partnership/co-marketing model. Website: stroomversnelling.nl.
- **Vakcentrum** — retail/smaller trades; lower ICP relevance.

## 6. Partner Program Compensation Structures

Dutch B2B referral partnerships in the SaaS/professional services space typically use one of the following models:

**Revenue share (meest voorkomend):**
- 10–20% of first-year MRR for each referred client that converts
- Sometimes 5–10% recurring for the lifetime of the contract (only if the partner has ongoing influence)
- Documented precedent: Simple-Simon pays SnelStart a "kickback-fee on license revenue" — exact % not disclosed but industry standard is 15%.

**Flat fee per signup:**
- €50–€250 one-time per qualified lead that converts to paying customer
- Lower admin burden; preferred by accountants and smaller introducers who dislike recurring tracking.

**Co-marketing only (no cash):**
- Common with branch organizations (Techniek Nederland, Bouwend Nederland): in exchange for branchepartner fee, the partner gets logo placement, newsletter mentions, and event access. No revenue share.
- Appropriate where the partner has fiduciary constraints (branch orgs cannot be seen as paid referrers of commercial products without member consent).

**Tiered hybrid:**
- Co-marketing entry + revenue share after proven volume. Example: Start with a €1,500/year Bouwend Nederland Netwerkpartner fee (co-marketing only), then upgrade to Voordeelpartner with a documented member discount that includes a referral component.

**Recommended structure for Klantkraan:**
- Branch orgs: co-marketing only (no revenue share; perceived neutrality is important)
- Software vendors: 15% of first-year MRR per converted referral + integration marketplace listing
- Accountants: €150 flat fee per converted BV customer, or 12-month 10% revenue share (their preference varies; offer both)
