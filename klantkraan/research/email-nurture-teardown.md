# Competitor Top-of-Funnel & Nurture Teardown — Klantkraan NL

**Date inspected:** 2026-05-21
**Scope:** NL competitors targeting trade BVs (installateur, loodgieter, dakdekker, aannemer, schilder) or productized-marketing SMB customers. Informs Klantkraan lead magnets, signup flow, and nurture sequence.

---

## Exec Summary (5 lines)

Every competitor in this market uses a single, frictionless CTA ("plan een gratis gesprek") rather than a gated lead magnet download. Risk reversal dominates: "20+ leads gegarandeerd, anders werken we gratis door" is the sector norm. Google reviews + client-count metrics are the dominant social proof; Klantenvertellen and Feedback Company appear on end-client trade sites but are absent from marketing agency homepages. Pricing is universally hidden, except the one subscription model (WebsiteEnMarketing) which publishes €29/€79/€129 tiers and stands out as the most transparent in the market. No competitor shows a visible email drip or content sequence — the nurture gap is real and Klantkraan can own it.

## 4. Trust Patterns Specific to Dutch B2B SMB Market

### 4.1 KvK number display

**Appears in:** LeadsMaster footer ("New Delphi Office Rijnzathe 6 NL-3454 PV Utrecht" + implied KvK); MB Effect (Pfinztalstraat 6, Leerdam address displayed).
**Verdict:** KvK number is legally required on websites per Dutch law. It functions as a baseline hygiene signal rather than a differentiator, but its absence is noticed. Display it in the footer.

### 4.2 Dutch review aggregators

**Klantenvertellen.nl:** Used extensively by end-client trade businesses (Installatie Techniek Beusichem, Beks Installatiebedrijf, Jan Veenstra, EB Loodgieters, Kapitein Loodgieter/Installateur) — confirms it is the dominant NL sector aggregator for trades.
**Trustpilot NL:** Used by VPM ("Uitstekend" label), LeadsMaster ("5.0 / 5 — 32 beoordelingen"), Grow Social Media.
**Google Reviews:** Used by MB Effect (60 reviews, 5/5), Rocket Marketing (45 reviews, 5.0), Cherry Marketing (4.3).
**Feedback Company:** Not seen in this sample.
**Verdict for Klantkraan:** Start on Google Reviews (fastest to accumulate in early stage), add Klantenvertellen when targeting end-trade customers as buyers.

### 4.3 Photo of team / eigenaar

**Appears in:** MB Effect homepage (team profiles section, 15+ specialists shown). Grow Social Media (implied by testimonial structure).
**Verdict:** Team photos are confirmed as an NL conversion factor per Dutch marketing literature ("menselijke foto's verhogen vertrouwen en conversie"). MB Effect's team section is the strongest in the sample. Include a founder photo in the Klantkraan hero or above-fold section.

### 4.4 Real address + phone number above the fold

**Above the fold:** Cherry Marketing (085 0270 270 in hero CTA). LeadsMaster (address in social proof section).
**Footer:** MB Effect (full address + phone). Multiple competitors display phone prominently.
**Verdict:** Phone number above the fold is a strong NL B2B trust signal, particularly for SMB trades who are phone-native buyers. Display a Dutch 085/088 number in the Klantkraan header.

### 4.5 WhatsApp CTA

**Appears in:** Topvakmannen ("Stuur een WhatsApp" as co-primary CTA).
**Verdict:** WhatsApp is the preferred async channel for Dutch SMB communication. A WhatsApp contact option alongside the standard form reduces friction for mobile-first trade owners.

### 4.6 Awards / keurmerken

**ICT Waarborg:** Cherry Marketing (installateur page).
**Google Partner:** Cherry Marketing, Roxtar.
**Meta Associate:** Roxtar.
**Thuiswinkel Zakelijk:** Not seen in this sample (relevant for e-commerce; less applicable to service).
**MKB-NL:** Not seen in this sample.
**Erkend Vakwerk (Casius):** End-client trade certification, not applicable to a marketing service.
**Verdict for Klantkraan:** Pursue Google Partner certification (standard credibility signal); consider ICT Waarborg if service includes website delivery; include a Techniek Nederland or UNETO-VNI logo (industry associations) if a partnership is established.

## 6. Klantkraan Recommendations

### 6.1 Top 3 lead magnets to ship first

**#1 — Gratis groei gesprek (15 minute intake call)**
Effort: Low (Cal.com booking embed, no content to produce).
Conversion: Highest in market — every competitor uses this; Dutch trade BV owners trust phone-first.
Format: Single CTA button linked to Cal.com; reinforce with "Geen verkooppraatje — concreet advies over jouw situatie."

**#2 — ROI calculator: "Wat levert marketing op voor een installatiebedrijf?"**
Effort: Medium (spreadsheet logic → Tally or custom embed).
Conversion: DakConnect proves the model works; no other marketing agency competitor in this sample offers one.
Inputs: gemiddeld klantbedrag, klanten per maand gewenst, huidig budget. Output: maandelijkse omzet upside.
Gate: Show result; collect name + email to receive full PDF breakdown.

**#3 — PDF gids: "5 manieren hoe installateurs en loodgieters meer klanten krijgen via Google in 2026"**
Effort: Low–Medium (1,500-word guide, 5 concrete tactics, one landing page).
Conversion: Medium–High; no competitor in sample offers a downloadable guide, so Klantkraan captures the search/content intent layer that is currently unoccupied.
Gate: Email + bedrijfsnaam only (2 fields max).

### 6.2 Landing page structure to copy

**Model: MB Effect (mbeffect.nl) + DakConnect (dakconnect.nl) hybrid**

MB Effect owns the diagnostic / trust-building pattern best:
Hero (rating badge + headline) → Problem statement in buyer's own words → Proof cluster (reviews + logos + metrics) → Method (3 steps) → FAQ → Dual CTA.

DakConnect adds the pricing / ROI transparency layer:
Interactive calculator above the fold + explicit per-unit pricing (or a "starting from" anchor) + "geen wurgcontracten" risk reversal.

Recommended Klantkraan page order:

1. Header: logo, phone, WhatsApp link
2. Hero: outcome headline + sub-headline (pain + promise) + single CTA ("Plan gratis gesprek")
3. Trust bar: Google stars + count + 2–3 logos
4. Pain section: 3-bullet problem statement in trade-owner voice
5. Calculator or ROI claim (concrete numbers)
6. Method: 3-step visual
7. Case studies: 2–3 named clients with before/after numbers
8. Risk reversal: guarantee statement
9. FAQ: 5–6 questions
10. Final CTA: form (bedrijfsnaam + email + telefoon + werkgebied)
11. Footer: KvK, BTW, adres, telefoon

### 6.3 Trust-pattern checklist for Klantkraan homepage + cornerstones

- [ ] Dutch phone number (085/088) in header — above the fold

| 3   | Day 7     | "Hoeveel kost het echt om een klant te werven via Google?"             | Cost-of-acquisition framing. Reference sector benchmarks (CPC, conversieratio, klantwaarde). Introduce the ROI calculator. |
| 4   | Day 12    | "Wat doet [bekende concurrent in jouw branche] dat jij nog niet doet?" | Social proof + FOMO. Share a (anonymised) case study of a trade client. CTA: boek een vrijblijvend gesprek.                |
| 5   | Day 18    | "Eén vraag: bent je al bezig met [specifiek probleem]?"                | Re-engagement / breakup email. Direct, short. Offer to unsubscribe or to book a call. Low-pressure close.                  |

All emails: plain text preferred (higher open rate in SMB; Dutch SMB buyers distrust HTML-heavy marketing). No emojis in subject lines for B2B trade segment.
