# Fast-cash research — 31 July 2026

**Brief:** first money in the bank within ~7 days, target €1–2k one-off, ≤€50 upfront, 10–25 hrs/week.
**Method:** 8 parallel research agents (freelance platforms, Dutch local, Klantkraan leverage, productized AI, paid research, digital products, content, wildcard) → 44 opportunities → 3 adversarial skeptic passes (payout latency, win probability, opportunity cost). ~130 web lookups, all claims cited in the full agent output.

## The one structural finding

**Payout latency disqualifies almost every platform.** Upwork: client approval → 5-day security hold → 2–4 business days to an EU bank = day 12–16 for a new account. Fiverr: hard 14-day new-seller clearing = day 18+. Gumroad: ~10–16 days. Lemon Squeezy: 2–3 weeks. Respondent: 7–10 business days post-session. Office Hours: 30 days post-call. Affiliate programs: 45–75 days.

Only four rails land money in a Dutch bank inside 7 days:

1. **Your own Mollie checkout** — iDEAL settles next business day. ⚠️ Mollie payouts default to weekly (Wednesday); switch to **daily payout** in the dashboard or the whole plan slips a week.
2. **Marktplaats** — cash/Tikkie at pickup, zero latency.
3. **Prolific** — £6 minimum cashout, PayPal ~24h (72h cooldown on first cashouts).
4. **Algora OSS bounties** — Stripe 1–3 days after merge, but sponsor + maintainer latency makes week-1 payout a tail outcome.

Conclusion: **the fastest path to €1–2k runs through the Mollie account you already have, selling things you already built.** Everything else is either a floor (tens of euros) or a weeks-2–4 pipeline.
