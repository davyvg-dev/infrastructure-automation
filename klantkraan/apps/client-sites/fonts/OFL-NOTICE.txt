This matters more here than on the marketing site: these files are copied into
every client's deploy, so the redistribution is real and this notice ships with
them (the fonts mount in astro.config.mjs copies it alongside the woff2 files).

Files are named <family>-<weight>-<subset>.woff2. Which family a given client
site ships is decided by stijl.letterontwerp (see fonts/manifest.json and
src/lib/stijl.ts); only that family's directory is copied into dist/fonts/.
