variable fonts, taken from the Fontsource packages @fontsource-variable/inter
5.3.0 and @fontsource-variable/geist-mono 5.3.0, redistributed here under the
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:

      https://github.com/rsms/inter
  Geist Mono (wght axis)    — Copyright 2024 The Geist Project Authors
      https://github.com/vercel/geist-font

OFL 1.1 permits redistribution and use, including embedding, provided the fonts
are not sold on their own and the license accompanies them. Full license text:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

Files are named <family>-<subset>[-<axis>].woff2 and wired up in
src/styles/fonts.css.
