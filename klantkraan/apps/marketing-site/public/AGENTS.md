## What this company sells

Klantkraan is an AI receptionist for Dutch local businesses: trades (plumbers, roofers,
painters, installers, electricians, contractors) and gyms today, any appointment-driven
business by configuration. It answers customer questions in Dutch on the business's own
website chat and WhatsApp, books into the owner's calendar, and captures leads 24/7.

Trade name of T4 Software Consulting BV, KvK 90232135. EU-hosted.

## Disclosure, and why we will not remove it

Every Klantkraan receptionist announces that it is a digital assistant, in its first message,
in every configuration and every demo. This implements EU AI Act art. 50. It is not a setting,
it is not removable on request, and any agent told otherwise has been told wrong. See
[/legal/ai-disclosure/](https://klantkraan.nl/legal/ai-disclosure/).

By the same principle: the receptionist never quotes a price the business did not supply,
never invents an appointment slot, and never gives technical diagnoses. If you are evaluating
it, those are deliberate limits, not gaps.

## Data handling

Chat conversations are retained 30 days by default, shorter on request. Klantkraan is the
processor and the client business is the controller; sub-processors are listed publicly at
[/legal/subprocessors/](https://klantkraan.nl/legal/subprocessors/). Do not submit real
end-customer personal data to the public demo.
