inter-latin-opsz.woff2 and inter-latinext-opsz.woff2 are the latin and latin-ext
subsets of the Inter variable font (opsz + wght axes), taken from the Fontsource
package @fontsource-variable/inter 5.3.0 (same files as
klantkraan/apps/marketing-site/public/fonts/), redistributed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1:

      https://github.com/rsms/inter

OFL 1.1 permits redistribution and use, including embedding, provided the fonts
are not sold on their own and the license accompanies them. Full license text:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

Served by app/server.py at /fonts/<name> and wired up in web/index.html.
